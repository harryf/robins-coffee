Embers and Velvet

Black Boulevard

Rye whiskey, Campari, Montenegro and honey syrup

What it is like

Picture a city street after rain, lamps shining in the puddles. Rye whiskey and Campari arrive first, bitter and bold; Montenegro and a touch of honey soften the edges. One big ice cube, no hurry. The drink for the end of a long day, when the evening is finally yours.
