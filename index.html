Stay a while

ROBIN's coffee was established in 2022 by Dennis Cobarrubias and the Sallahi family.

By day it is the calm corner of the Old Town: a coffee with a friend, a book, an hour of work. By night the lights go down, the cocktails come out and the room fills with people you haven't met yet.
