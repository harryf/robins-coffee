# What's new in ROBIN's Studio

The newest version comes first. Each version says what changed for the person using the Studio.

## 0.12.0

21 November 2026

- The menu has seasons. A season has a name, a first and a last day, and a few words about it. The site shows the season that runs today.
- Add, change and remove seasons in Menu. A season can only be removed when no drink is in it.
- A drink can be in one season or in several. Tick its seasons in the drink's form.
- A drink now has more fields, in this order: name, price, short line, what drinking it is like, ingredients (amount, measure, what), how to make it, and pictures.
- A drink can have several pictures. The first is the one on the menu card; add real photos after it, each with its alt text. Move them up or down, or take one off.
- Some texts are marked "Draft by Claude: please check it". Read them, change what is wrong, and save: the mark goes.
- Every drink has its own page on the site, and the menu page lists every season.

## 0.11.0

21 November 2026

- When the Studio opens, it shows the ROBIN's logo and says what it is doing: checking for updates, installing a new version, starting.
- After an update, the first screen says which version you now have, with a link to this page. Close the note and it stays away.
- Settings shows the version number, the day it was installed on this Mac, and a link to what is new in it.

## 0.10.1

21 November 2026

- The Studio speaks English only.
- You type a text for the site in English. When you save, this Mac translates it into German, French and Italian. Nothing leaves the Mac.
- Under each English field, a closed fold shows the translations. You can open it and change them.
- If a translation language is missing on this Mac, the Studio says so once and shows how to add it.
- Settings lists the translation languages this Mac has.
- The phone editor is gone: the Studio is the one place to edit the site.

## 0.9.5

21 November 2026

- History lists only content changes: hours, photos, albums, drinks and events. Each one says what changed and links to its page on GitHub.
- Preview pages say "Local preview" in the tab and in a small mark in the corner, so they are not taken for the live site.
- Publish shows a clock while it runs and says how long it usually takes.

## 0.9.4

21 November 2026

- An ordinary edit (hours, a price, a caption, a removed event or photo) no longer fails the check before going live.
- What you publish is described in plain words: which photo, caption, album, drink, price, event or hours changed.
- A GitHub key without the right permissions gets its own message, which names the two permissions it needs.

## 0.9.3

21 November 2026

- The Studio can be installed on a Mac from one line on the install page.
- The ROBIN's logo is the app's icon and sits beside the name in the sidebar.
- You can rename an album from the Photos screen. Its photos stay in it.
- A picture made for a drink or an event is deleted when nothing uses it any more.

## 0.9.1

20 November 2026

- Safety fixes from a second look at the code. A step of an update that failed is tried again at the next start.

## 0.9.0

20 November 2026

- Photos and albums are one screen: pictures shown album by album, with "Add a photo" and "New album" at the top.
- Menu: each drink with its picture, and one form to add or change it.
- Events: "Add an event" at the top, by hand or from Eventfrog, and a calendar of everything coming up.
- History: "Restore this version" brings the site's content back to an earlier day.
- Settings: the version, the folder the app edits, and the tools of this Mac.
- The Studio updates itself every time it starts. Your unpublished changes stay as they are.
